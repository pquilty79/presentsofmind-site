Pick an occasion, write your welcome, and add as many clues as you like. Include a photo, a hint, or a family inside joke.

Choose your hiding places and final reveal. Add an optional countdown for the date and time your adventure should begin.

A nudge for your imagination

Stuck on the
first riddle?

Tell the writing assistant about their interests and reading level, then choose real hiding places. Keep, edit, or try again until the clues sound right.

Optional Apple Intelligence writes on a compatible device running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Editable templates are available without AI. You review every clue before adding it.

No app accounts, advertising SDKs, or third-party analytics. The developer doesn’t receive your clues, photos, or writing-assistant details. You decide whether to share a hunt.
