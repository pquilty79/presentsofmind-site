Presents of Mind / Privacy policy

Personal adventures.
Private by design.

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Presents of Mind iPhone/iPad app and its support website handle information. The developer is Patrick Quilty.

The short version

The app works without an account. Your hunts, photos, progress, and writing notes are stored on your device. The app does not send them to the developer, run advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or sell your information. You choose when to share a hunt.

Information you use in the app

You may enter a hunt title, recipient or occasion name, riddles, accepted answers, hints, final messages, dates and times, and optional photos or artwork. The writing assistant can also store optional age or reading level, relationship, interests, gifts, and a planned route. These are used to create and play your hunt and to save drafts or progress locally. Names and exact ages are not required.

These entries are not collected by the developer or uploaded to a developer-operated server. The app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, or full photo library.

Photos and device access

Apple’s photo picker lets you select the images you want to include. The app processes selected images on the device and includes their prepared copies in saved hunts and shared files. Images can contain personal details visible in the picture itself, so choose them with care. Selecting a photo does not upload it to us.

Optional on-device AI

On supported devices, the writing assistant uses Apple’s Foundation Models framework and its on-device system model. Reading level, relationship, interests, the occasion, tone, difficulty, and the particular target location may be provided to that local model to draft a riddle. Presents and the final destination are excluded from model prompts. The app does not send prompts or responses to the developer or to a cloud AI provider.

Apple Intelligence must be available and enabled, and its model may require a system-managed download. Editable templates are available if you do not use AI. You can turn off Apple Intelligence use in the writing assistant; this stops future AI requests from that assistant. Existing writing notes and suggestions remain in your local draft unless removed. Apple’s own system services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Sharing a hunt

When you choose Share hunt, the app creates a protected player file and opens the system share sheet. That file contains the hunt information and images needed for play. Answers, later stages, and the final reveal are protected with encryption; author-only writing notes and unused suggestions are removed. The opening clue and public hunt details remain readable.

You decide the recipient and sharing method. AirDrop, Messages, email, Files, cloud storage, or other services you choose may process a copy under their own policies. These are user-directed sharing choices, not automatic transfers to our servers. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely erase a file you have sent. Encryption does not make short or predictable answers impossible to guess.

Local storage and backups

Author hunts, unfinished writing, received hunts, earned progress, and preferences are stored locally. They remain until you change or remove them, delete the app’s data, or remove the app. Compatibility copies from earlier app versions may remain in the app’s local storage. The app does not provide developer-hosted synchronization.

App data may be included in iCloud or computer backups according to your device settings. Backups are handled by Apple or your chosen backup system; the developer has no access to them. Deleting a hunt from the active library does not erase older device backups, exported files, or copies on another device.

App purchase

Presents of Mind is a paid app with a US App Store price of $0.99. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple handles the App Store purchase; the app does not receive payment card information.

Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

The app does not include advertising networks, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated telemetry service. We do not sell or rent personal information. Apple may separately process App Store, device, purchase, or diagnostic information according to your system settings and its own privacy practices.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include. We use that correspondence to respond, investigate the reported issue, and handle privacy requests. Email is handled through our email provider, Google, under its privacy policy. Do not send passwords, a child’s personal details, private hunt answers, or photos unless they are necessary and you have chosen to share them.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to respond, resolve the issue, and meet applicable obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence using Email Presents of Mind Support. We do not have access to your device’s local hunts and cannot delete those on your behalf.

This website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded social widgets, cookies, or a contact form. The site’s images and styles are hosted with the site.

GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser information, and requested pages, to deliver and secure its hosting service. Visiting an external link or using email is subject to that provider’s practices. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children and families

The app is designed for a parent or another gift-giver to create a hunt and share it with a recipient. It does not require a child to create an account or submit personal information to the developer. Adults control the details and images included in the hunt and should avoid unnecessary personal information. A parent or guardian can contact support about privacy without including a child’s name or other identifying details.

Your choices and deletion

  • Edit or delete hunts: use the app’s editing controls, trash button, or swipe-left delete action. Deletion removes a hunt and its associated draft from the active on-device library.
  • Remove local app data: delete the app through iOS/iPadOS settings rather than choosing Offload App. This removes its local app container, including older compatibility copies.
  • Remove other copies: separately delete exported files, attachments, files saved in cloud storage, and copies on other devices. Manage or remove device backups through your Apple or computer backup settings.
  • Stop optional AI: turn off Use Apple Intelligence in the writing assistant or use templates. You can also manage Apple Intelligence in system Settings.
  • Manage sharing: do not export a hunt unless you want to send its player content. Previously shared copies remain with their recipients.
  • Contact us: request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence through Email Presents of Mind Support. There is no developer-hosted app account to delete.

Changes and contact

If the app’s practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to Patrick Quilty through Email Presents of Mind Support.
