Creating your hunt

How do I get started?

Choose Create a hunt, set the occasion, and add your clues in order. For each riddle, enter at least one accepted answer. Add optional hints or photos, then write a final message revealing the surprise. Use the original example hunt for inspiration.

Can I use a birthday, wedding, or another holiday?

Yes. Choose a preset or type your own occasion name. Artwork matches the occasion automatically, or you can upload your own. Christian holiday presets offer Gregorian and Julian calendar choices; Julian Pascha finishes with “Christ is Risen!”

Do I have to use a countdown?

No. Leave Countdown off to play anytime. When enabled, you can change the unlock date and time. Holiday presets suggest an upcoming observance, but traditions and local dates can differ, especially for lunar holidays. Check the date for your family. The countdown uses the device clock.

Can I save, edit, or reuse a hunt?

Your writing saves on this device as a draft. Return to Continue writing to resume. Finished hunts can be edited or reused from the library. Choose Finished when you are ready to save the completed version and share it. Reusing a hunt creates a separate copy with fresh progress.

Getting help with riddles

How does “Help me write a hunt” work?

In the editor, choose Help me write a hunt. Add optional interests, reading level, relationship, tone, and difficulty. Choose your clue locations in order and the final present location. The assistant suggests up to 12 clues at a time; you can add more in the main editor. Keep, edit, skip, or regenerate each suggestion. Existing clues are kept.

Why am I seeing templates instead of AI?

On-device writing needs iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, a device that supports Apple Intelligence, Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings, and its model downloaded and ready. Language and regional availability can vary. The assistant explains availability and offers editable templates. You can also turn AI off yourself. Replace any bracketed writing prompts before keeping those clues.

Does AI cost extra or send personal details to a server?

The app’s writing feature uses Apple’s on-device model. It does not make a paid cloud AI call or send your details to the developer. The model may need an initial system-managed download. Gifts and the final hiding place are reserved for the final message and are left out of AI prompts. Always check that generated clues are clear and appropriate.

Sharing and playing

How do I send a hunt to another device?

Choose Finished → Share hunt, then use AirDrop, Messages, or another available sharing option. The receiving device needs Presents of Mind installed. Open the .presenthunt attachment with the app, or save it to Files and choose Open a shared hunt. Photos travel inside the file. Creation and play work offline; some sharing methods need a connection.

Can someone read the file to see the answers?

New shared files protect the answers and later stages with encryption. Correct answers unlock the trail step by step. The first clue remains readable, and a self-contained offline file cannot prevent determined guessing or device-clock changes. Don’t treat a hunt as secure storage for sensitive information.

A shared hunt won’t open. What should I try?

Check that Presents of Mind is installed and that you have the complete attachment. Save it to Files and try importing from inside the app. Protected older .xmashunt files remain supported, but the earliest plaintext file format is rejected; ask the creator to share a fresh protected copy. Large photo hunts must stay within the app’s 40 MiB file limit.

Does answer capitalization matter?

No. Answers ignore capitalization, surrounding spaces, and accents. The answer still needs to match one of the accepted alternatives. The creator can add alternatives such as “sofa” and “couch.”

Can I turn off sound or reduce animations?

Use the sound control while playing. Sounds respect the device’s Silent mode. The app also respects the system’s Reduce Motion setting.

Storage, backup, and deletion

Are hunts synced or backed up?

Hunts are saved locally, not live-synced between devices by this app. Their inclusion in iCloud or computer device backups depends on your Apple settings. A shared player file is not an editable author backup. Check your backup settings before changing devices or deleting the app.

How do I delete a hunt or my information?

Use its trash button or swipe left in the library and confirm deletion. This removes the hunt from the active library on that device. Copies you exported, sent to others, saved elsewhere, or retained in backups need to be deleted separately. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. There is no developer-hosted app account to delete. See your privacy choices for details.
